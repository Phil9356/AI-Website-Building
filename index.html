  1. 1

    Pick

    Pickling cucumbers come from two farms in the Willamette Valley, picked small and firm.

  2. 2

    Pack

    Each crock is packed by hand with dill, garlic and spices the same day.

  3. 3

    Ferment

    Sea salt brine and 21 days at cellar temperature turn them sour on their own.

  4. 4

    Jar

    Jarred raw and kept cold, so the live cultures are still there when you open it.
